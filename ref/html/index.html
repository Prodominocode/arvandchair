Arvand since 1960

Arvand opened as a small studio workshop in 1960, built on one rule that has never changed: nothing leaves the bench until it's right. Six decades on, that same discipline — patient, exacting and quietly inventive — still guides every curve, weld and stitch we make.

Philosophy

We believe comfort is never accidental. Every curve is shaped by a decade of posture research, every material chosen to outlast trends by years, and every joint built so a worn part is a five-minute fix, not a reason to replace the whole chair. Precision is not a finish we apply at the end — it's the discipline we start with.
